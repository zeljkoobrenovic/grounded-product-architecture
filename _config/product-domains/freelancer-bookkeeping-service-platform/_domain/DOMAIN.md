# Freelancer Bookkeeping Service Platform

This product domain models the business operated by **Kees de Boekhouder** (keesdeboekhouder.nl; vortexcp.com/investment/kees-de-boekhouder/): an Amsterdam-based bookkeeping-as-a-service company founded in 2009 by Kees Monteban and Marlou ter Berg for freelancers who found existing solutions "too complex and overpriced". The company combines a proprietary web and mobile application with a **personal, internally trained bookkeeper** on a fixed yearly subscription, invoiced monthly. It serves more than 10,000 entrepreneurs from offices in Amsterdam, Rotterdam and Utrecht, with a team of roughly 100 people of whom about 40 percent are bookkeepers. Vortex Capital Partners took a stake in a buyout announced on 24 May 2022 (then 6,500+ clients) to build the brand into the national go-to bookkeeper for freelancers; Vortex reports a revenue CAGR above 25 percent and has focused on reinforcing leadership, organisational structure and the professionalisation of the technology function. BV clients (holding and operating companies) are served by the sister company **Founders** (Founders Finance B.V., founded 2018, same address) at the same fixed-fee model. It is an instructive example of **tech-enabled services**: software that captures the administration at the source, a human review and filing layer that carries the fiscal responsibility, and a brand that grows through community and word of mouth.

## Domain boundary

**Core scope.** Everything a Dutch micro-entrepreneur needs to go from receipt and invoice to filed tax return on one subscription, following the Kees de Boekhouder proposition:

- **Client administration app** (mijn.keesdeboekhouder.nl and mobile) — a dashboard with direct insight into revenue per quarter, cost categories, investments, profit and the VAT to pay or receive per quarter, updated in real time as bookings are submitted; Scan & Herken for photographing, uploading and recognising receipts and purchase invoices; quotes and invoices with status tracking; document storage; multiple trading names per entity; deadline reminders.
- **Bank connection** — a PSD2 connection through Ponto (Isabel Group) to every Ponto-connected bank (Knab, Rabobank, ABN AMRO, bunq and others), synchronising transactions four times a day, matching them automatically against bookings, with manual matching or direct booking for the rest, re-authorisation every 90 to 180 days, priced at €5 per month per connected account.
- **Bookkeeping engine and filings** — the bookkeeper checks the administration, files the quarterly VAT return, prepares the annual accounts and files the income tax return (for a partner: €125 incl. VAT); for VOFs the profit split per partner; for BVs through Founders the corporate tax return, statutory annual accounts and KVK filing; payroll at €17.50 per payslip per employee per month. Filings go to the Belastingdienst through the official digital channels.
- **Bookkeeper workspace** — the review queue and client file per bookkeeper, quality assurance and four-eyes checks, allocation of clients to bookkeepers across three offices with colleagues as backup, the filing calendar, client messaging by e-mail and phone with video or in-person meetings on request, and the advice knowledge base (deductions such as zelfstandigenaftrek and startersaftrek, mkb-winstvrijstelling, KOR, the hours criterion, buying a house as a freelancer, entrepreneurship next to a job, Wet DBA).
- **Client lifecycle** — a free introduction call via Google Meet, a mutual-fit check, account access and bookkeeper assignment, switching from a previous bookkeeper with digital copies of old returns, fixed-fee subscription plans (eenmanszaak €840, VOF €1,140 for two partners plus €100 per extra partner, holding BV €1,750, werkmaatschappij €2,450, all ex VAT per year and invoiced monthly), a first full fiscal year commitment and monthly cancellation with one calendar month notice afterwards.
- **Partners and community** — referral benefits with professional associations and service providers (BNO, DuPho, Kunstenbond, Stichting BOK, voordekunst, Unfair, Insify, BrightPensioen, DAS), entrepreneur network profiles, client stories and video testimonials, blog and "Keep up" content.
- **Foundation** — identity and access, web application and mobile access, audit trail, AVG (GDPR) compliant data handling with access restricted to authorised staff, integrations (Ponto, tax authority, KVK, e-mail, payments).

**Adjacent scope (modeled lightly).** Payroll for small employers; business insurance, pensions and legal counsel through partners; financing (BridgeFund lists Kees as an expert partner). Corporate entities are modeled through the Founders sister brand as one customer segment and one product, not as a separate domain.

**Explicitly excluded.** Kees de Boekhouder is not a self-service accounting software vendor (external accounting packages are not accepted), not a bank or payment institution (business IBANs and cards as offered by Tellow or Moneybird are out of scope), not an accountancy firm for audited annual accounts, not a tax-advice boutique for wealthy individuals, and not a staffing or freelance marketplace. This distinguishes the domain from `payments-and-revenue-infrastructure` (money movement), `travel-and-expense-management` (corporate expense workflows) and `transfer-pricing-compliance-platform` (multinational tax compliance). Here the business is fixed yearly subscriptions per legal form, small add-ons (bank connection, partner income tax return, payroll per payslip), and the labour productivity of bookkeepers on top of software.

## Value exchange

- **Freelancers and sole proprietors** (creative professionals such as designers, photographers, stylists, makers and artists; knowledge workers such as consultants and developers; starters combining a first business with a job) pay €70 per month and get a personal bookkeeper who checks their administration, files VAT and income tax on time, answers questions all year and updates them proactively about changes and deadlines, plus an app that makes receipts, invoices and bank transactions almost effortless. Their scarce resource is time and peace of mind, not accounting skill.
- **VOF partners and BV directors** get the same fixed-fee, no-surprise model for partnerships (profit split, partner returns) and companies (corporate tax, statutory accounts, DGA payroll through Founders).
- **The company's own bookkeepers and client-success staff** operate the same platform: internally trained on the technology and the entrepreneurial method, each bookkeeper carries a portfolio of clients, and the workspace decides how many clients one bookkeeper can serve well.
- **Partners** (professional associations, insurers, pension and legal providers) reach a concentrated audience of freelancers with member benefits and referral discounts, and the community lowers Kees' acquisition cost.
- **The strategic asset** is the combination: software that captures administration at the source with recognised receipts and matched bank transactions, and a trained, personal human layer that carries fiscal responsibility and trust. Loyal customers become brand ambassadors (Vortex), and the fixed transparent price is the promise that the brand must keep as it scales from 10,000 to 20,000 clients.

## Strategic spine (1/3/5-year horizons)

- **Year 1 — Scale the proven model without losing the personal bookkeeper.** Double toward 20,000 clients through data-driven growth (segments, channels, partnerships), professionalise the technology function under Vortex, raise bookkeeper productivity with better recognition, bank matching and review tooling, and keep filing on time as the Belastingdienst enforces the Wet DBA and the zelfstandigenaftrek shrinks.
- **Year 3 — The national go-to brand for freelancer bookkeeping.** Bookkeeper capacity planned as a product, near-fully automated administration for standard sole proprietors with human review by exception, a seamless path from starter to VOF to BV (Founders) on one platform, e-invoicing and Peppol readiness ahead of EU ViDA, and partner integrations (insurance, pension, financing) that make Kees the financial home of the freelancer.
- **Year 5 — Continuous, AI-assisted bookkeeping with a human guarantee.** Real-time tax position and reservation advice, agentic booking and drafting of returns supervised by bookkeepers, proactive fiscal advice as a differentiator against software-only competitors, and selective expansion into adjacent micro-business segments or geographies.
