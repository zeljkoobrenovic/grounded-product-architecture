# Working Notes

Domain choice: Internet Number Registry and Routing Trust.

Rationale:

- RIPE NCC's most prominent activity is acting as the Regional Internet Registry for IPv4, IPv6, and AS Number resources in its service region.
- The domain also includes services explicitly listed by RIPE NCC: LIR Portal, RIPE Database, RPKI, K-root, DNS services, RIPE Atlas, RIPEstat, RIS, RIPE IPmap, research, and community outreach.
- Peer comparison is mainly with the other RIRs. Commercial adjacency comes from DNS registry operators, DDoS/CDN/security networks, IP address marketplaces, and managed DNS providers.

Structural references:

- `cloud-data-and-ai-platform` for a current compact infrastructure-domain source bundle.
- `public-cloud-services` for platform, operations, and commercial ecosystem decomposition.
- `enterprise-architecture-management-platform` for sourced competition structure with scope caveats.

Assumptions:

- KPIs that are not directly public are seed operating targets, not reported RIPE NCC commitments.
- Competitor metrics preserve the scope reported by the source, so peer RIR membership counts, DNS registry domain base, CDN/security revenue, and IP marketplace inventory are intentionally not normalized.
