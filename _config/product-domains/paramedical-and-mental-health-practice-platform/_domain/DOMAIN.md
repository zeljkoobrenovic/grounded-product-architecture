# Paramedical and Mental Health Practice Platform

This product domain models the business operated by **Health Cloud Initiative (HCI)** (hci-software.com; vortexcp.com/investment/hci/): a Dutch healthcare software group that delivers practice management systems and electronic patient records (EPD), patient environments and e-health to paramedical practices (physiotherapy, exercise therapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, dietetics, foot care, rehabilitation) and to mental health care (GGZ) practices and institutions, and that connects them with general practitioners, pharmacies and hospitals through the Uw Zorg Online patient app. Its mission is "Better care, made easy" ("meer tijd voor zorg door meer gemak voor zorgverleners én patiënten"). HCI supports more than 40,000 healthcare professionals every day and more than 4 million patients, with a team of about 250 people in the Netherlands and Switzerland, and is certified for NEN 7510 and ISO 27001.

Vortex Capital Partners partnered with founder Eric de Boer (Incura, formerly Winbase) in a 2021 buyout to execute a **buy-and-build** strategy. At the time of the June 2022 announcement HCI served 7,500+ healthcare professionals, processed more than €500 million in annual declarations, had a team of 45 and reached 2+ million patients; the stated ambition was to relieve the administrative burden of 20,000 professionals. Since then Vortex has supported more than 14 add-on acquisitions: Incura, CRS (GGZ EPD), FysioRoadmap, Winbase, Ensemble/Evry and Footfit/PodoFile (January 2023), NICE Software with paraNICE and NICE Connect (May 2023), Uw Zorg Online (merged June 2023), Quli (September 2023), Praktijkinfo practice websites (October 2023), Embloom (July 2024), SpotOnMedics (August 2024, from VvAA), plus Trompbx, LogicData/FysioLogic, Abakus and Edevop. Turnover was reported at €20 million in late 2023. Marc Prette led the company as CEO for two years; Robin Dunki Jacobs, who joined in 2024 as Business Unit Director responsible for integrating the labels, became CEO on 1 May 2025. The current strategic programme is **HCI One**: a new multidisciplinary paramedical platform built on the web-based FysioRoadmap foundation that consolidates FysioRoadmap, SpotOnMedics, Incura, Evry, Abakus, Podofile, Edevop and FysioLogic, rolled out per discipline (physiotherapy first) with no forced migration deadline.

It is an instructive example of a **regulated vertical SaaS roll-up**: many acquired labels with loyal user bases, one shared compliance and integration backbone (Vecozo declarations, ZorgDomein referrals, MedMij data exchange, NEN 7510 security), a consumer-scale patient front door, and a multi-year platform consolidation that must not disturb daily care.

## Domain boundary

**Core scope.** Everything a Dutch paramedical or mental health care provider needs to plan, record, measure, declare and communicate care, and everything a patient needs to take part digitally:

- **Practice management and EPD for paramedics** (HCI One, FysioRoadmap, SpotOnMedics, Incura, Evry, ParaNICE) — the agenda as "the central heart of the practice" across therapists and locations, 24/7 online booking synchronised with the agenda, group lessons and subscriptions, the web-based patient dossier with customisable care pathways per discipline and guideline-based templates (KNGF), voice-to-text documentation, measurement instruments and PROMs, automated declarations generated from the agenda and submitted to insurers through Vecozo (GDS801 replaced PM304 on 1 January 2025), patient invoicing with Mollie payments, outsourced billing services that handle return messages, management dashboards (production, revenue, treatment duration, referrers), and monthly pseudonymised data delivery to the Landelijke Database Fysiotherapie (LDF) and the Keurmerk practice register.
- **Mental health care EPD** (HCI CRS) — the all-in-one EPD for GGZ institutions, practices and youth care: client dossiers, treatment plans and multidisciplinary coordination, medication, agenda, registration and declaration under the zorgprestatiemodel (from 2026 every declaration carries a mandatory referral date), Jeugdwet and WMO contracting with municipalities, a client portal, SmartAI voice-to-text, reporting and analytics, and integrations with Embloom, Bergop, Therapieland, Minddistrict, ZorgDomein and ZorgMail.
- **Patient environments** (Uw Zorg Online, Quli) — the digital front door for GP practices, pharmacies, hospitals and therapists: e-consult and secure messaging, appointment booking in the practice agenda, repeat prescriptions and the medication overview, access to records, treatment plans and correspondence, self-measurements and diaries, digital triage ("Moet ik naar de dokter?"), connections to the GP and pharmacy information systems (Sanday, HealthConnected, Pharmapartners, CGM, Bricks, Vertimart) and MedMij-certified personal health environments (PGO) with granular sharing and network care for family and informal carers (Quli).
- **E-health** (Embloom, Emogy, NICE Connect) — a library of 500+ questionnaires and 450 e-health interventions fully integrated in the EPD, blended-care programmes and monitoring for GGZ, POH-GGZ and somatic care, video consultation and remote care trajectories (NICE Connect, in use since 2014 with Amsterdam UMC), exercise-programme partners (Physitrack, Physiotrack, Fysio AI) and the Emogy client app for cognitive and behavioural support.
- **Network and integration** — referral and secure messaging (ZorgDomein, ZorgMail), HIS/AIS connectors, insurance eligibility checks and declarations (Vecozo), quality data (LDF, Qualiview, MediQuest), accounting (Twinfield), payments (Mollie), a partner API and open platform, practice websites (Praktijkinfo portfolio under Uw Zorg Online) and the Zorgverlener App.
- **Foundation** — identity and access for professionals and patients, audit logging, consent and information security under NEN 7510 and ISO 27001, multi-tenant cloud runtime, data migration tooling for label consolidation into HCI One, and customer onboarding, training and support.

**Adjacent scope (modeled lightly).** GP and pharmacy practice software itself (HCI connects to HIS/AIS systems, it does not replace them), dental care, hospital rehabilitation departments (paraNICE supports hospital information systems), German rehabilitation centres (NICE Connect) and the Swiss activities.

**Explicitly excluded.** HCI is not a hospital EPD vendor (ChipSoft, Epic, Nexus for hospitals), not a long-term care (VVT) or disability care ECD (Nedap Ons, SDB), not a health insurer or clearing house (Vecozo, Vektis), not a telehealth provider employing clinicians, and not a medical device or wearable company. This distinguishes the domain from `digital-medication-management` (pharmacy medication workflows), `mental-wellbeing-community-platform` (consumer wellbeing communities) and `occupational-health-and-safety-platform` (occupational medicine). Here the business is recurring software subscriptions per practice or professional, custom-quoted per number of therapists, modules and integrations, plus billing services and e-health content, on top of consumer-scale patient environments that are free for citizens and paid by care providers.

## Value exchange

- **Physiotherapists and allied health therapists** (speech, occupational and exercise therapists, dietitians) get an agenda, dossier and declaration flow that runs from appointment to paid claim with as little typing as possible, guideline-compliant records that pass audits and quality registers, and a patient app that reduces phone calls. Their scarce resource is direct treatment time: physiotherapists report about a full working day per week on administration, and reimbursement rates are low.
- **Independent GGZ practitioners and GGZ institutions** get an EPD built for the zorgprestatiemodel and municipal youth care contracts, ROM and questionnaires integrated in the treatment, blended care with e-health modules, a client portal and analytics, in a market where waiting times exceed the 14-week Treeknorm in almost every region and regulators demand more data.
- **Patients and citizens** get one place (Uw Zorg Online or Quli) to reach their GP, pharmacy, therapist or GGZ institution, book, ask, order repeat medication, read their record and share it under their own control.
- **GP practices and pharmacies** get a digital front door that absorbs routine contact and connects to their own information systems, plus practice websites.
- **Integration partners** (exercise apps, questionnaire providers, quality registers, accounting, payments, HIS/AIS vendors, insurers via Vecozo) reach a large installed base through one open platform.
- **HCI's own implementation, migration and support staff** operate the same platform to onboard practices, migrate acquired labels into HCI One and keep support responsive.
- **The strategic asset** is the combination of an installed base of 40,000+ professionals across many disciplines, a consumer front door with 4 million users, and the integrations and certifications that every practice needs but no single practice can maintain. The promise Vortex and HCI must keep is that consolidation into HCI One delivers "the best of every label" without disrupting daily care.

## Strategic spine (1/3/5-year horizons)

- **Year 1 — Integrate the labels and keep every declaration flowing.** Roll HCI One out for physiotherapy, keep FysioRoadmap, SpotOnMedics, Incura and Evry fully supported, absorb the 2025 GDS801 declaration standard and the 2026 zorgprestatiemodel changes without rejection spikes, ship voice-to-text and AI documentation, and raise online booking, e-consult and portal activation so that practices feel the administrative relief.
- **Year 3 — One multidisciplinary platform with the patient in the middle.** All paramedical disciplines live on HCI One; CRS, Embloom and Quli form one integrated GGZ proposition; Uw Zorg Online is the MedMij-connected front door across GP, pharmacy, paramedical and GGZ care; digital triage and AI assistants take routine work from practices; the partner API becomes the standard way to add e-health content and devices.
- **Year 5 — The care network platform for primary and mental health care in the Benelux and DACH.** Data-driven care pathways and outcome benchmarks, prevention and remote monitoring programmes reimbursed by insurers, and selective expansion of NICE Connect, Embloom and the Swiss activities, with the platform positioned as the answer to labour shortages and administrative burden in first-line care.
