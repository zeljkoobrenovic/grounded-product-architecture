# Energy Market Operations Platform

This product domain models the business operated by **Eneve** (eneve.com, vortexcp.com/investment/eneve/): a Netherlands-headquartered energy software and expertise-services group formed in 2025 when **Energy21** (Utrecht, founded 1997 as GEN; EBASE platform for BRP market messages, real-time production, consumption and balancing data), **Ecedo** (Dutch, standardised SaaS for energy suppliers since 2003, full SaaS platform 2013: CRM, registration, market communication and billing), **Jules** (Loughborough, founded 2010/2011; flexible digital contract, forecast and customer-trading self-service for suppliers and business customers in the Netherlands and the UK) and **Gridhub** (Rotterdam, founded 2023; white-label order processing, tariff management and customer portals) were consolidated into one brand. **Vortex Capital Partners** (Amsterdam) bought into Energy21 in 2022 and financed the add-ons; on 30 March 2026 Eneve acquired **Nemon** (Ascó, Catalonia; Nemon Trade Energy, Trade Energy CRM and Trade Energy SIPS; about 100 supplier customers in Spain and Portugal, roughly 30 specialists), taking the group to about 180 people and an expected turnover above EUR 30 million. It is an instructive example of **vertical operations software for a regulated, real-time market** where the software vendor also operates a market role (BRP as a Service) on behalf of its customers.

## Domain boundary

**Core scope.** Everything an energy supplier, BRP, trader or portfolio operator needs to run the lifecycle *Propose → Onboard → Measure → Bill → Serve → Source → Forecast → Nominate → Steer → Settle* on one platform, following Eneve's three suites:

- **Supplier Suite** — Customer Onboarding (automated intake and activation flows), Contract & Billing (every contract and product type: fixed, variable, dynamic, EV, battery and bundled offers, complex B2B multi-site agreements; automated billing runs with VAT, guarantees of origin and split invoicing; reconciliation and exception handling; smart-meter, telemetry and clustered data), Customer Interfacing (white-label web and mobile portal with invoice breakdown, consumption history, projected costs, tariff changes, meter readings, disputes and service requests; customer lifecycle and touchpoints) and Sourcing & Pricing (dynamic pricing and procurement tools, position tracking, portfolio forecasting, fine-grained portfolio structuring). Plus the low-code proposition builder ("From idea to live product. In weeks."): commercial teams configure products, tariffs, journeys, pricing logic, campaigns and sales-channel availability that plug into the order-to-cash backbone automatically.
- **Balancing & Shipping Suite** — Intraday Steering (real-time signals and adjustments, solar-optimised steering, near real-time position updates), BRP as a Service (fully managed balance responsibility: day-ahead, intraday and ex-post forecasting, TenneT nominations and re-nominations, ETPA intraday trading run as a guaranteed cycle, allocation, imbalance management, reconciliation and settlement; "up to 53% reduction in imbalance costs", portfolios from 40 GWh, tiered MWh pricing) and Balancing & Shipping (automated nominations and position management for electricity and gas, including gas trading and shipping for EBN).
- **Trade & Portfolio Suite** — Customer Trading (executing and managing customer energy trades, click contracts and forecast management for business customers, the Jules heritage) with portfolio position, price and risk visibility ("Track positions, spot risks, take action").
- **Market and data foundation** — Dutch market communication (EDSN message flows, central connection register, TenneT market-to-market communication hub introduced with Allocation 2.0 tranche 3 on 1 January 2025, the 15-minute day-ahead market since 30 September 2025), Iberian market communication (SIPS, ATR, OMIE, distributor switching files), operational dashboards with 15-minute financial insight per segment, regulatory reporting, identity and multi-tenant brand isolation, workflow and exception handling, the open API and integration connectors, and migration tooling that consolidates five heritage products onto one platform.
- **Expertise services** — Energy Market Enablement (market-entry scan for the Netherlands and Spain: regulatory scan, competitive overview, operating model, commercial blueprint and roadmap in five weeks; ACM and CNMC licensing support of 8–20 weeks), Eneve Academy training, and Customer Services (implementation, strategy guidance and ongoing support).

**Adjacent scope (modeled lightly).** Flexibility and asset steering for batteries, EV and solar; grid-operator use of validated consumption data (Eneve's software validates over 90% of Dutch energy consumption data); expansion into Germany, Belgium and the UK.

**Explicitly excluded.** The platform operator generates, sells or trades no energy for its own account: no energy retail brand, no generation assets, no grid ownership, no metering hardware, no consumer price-comparison site. This distinguishes the domain from `emobility` (charging networks) and `public-cloud-services`. Here the business is software subscriptions per supplier or portfolio (scaling with connections and MWh), managed BRP services priced per MWh, implementation, market-entry projects, academy training and support.

## Value exchange

- **Energy suppliers** (Eneco, Essent, Engie, Vattenfall, Mega, Vandebron, Scholt Energie, Pure Energie, PZEM in the Netherlands; about 100 suppliers in Spain and Portugal including Barcelona Energia) pay a subscription that scales with connections and modules. They get one operational backbone from proposition to cash, propositions launched in weeks instead of eighteen months, straight-through billing with minimal corrections, customer self-service on the same data as billing and settlement, and market-communication compliance kept current by the vendor.
- **BRPs, traders and portfolio operators** (Axpo, TotalEnergies, EBN, Pure Energie and the suppliers' own portfolio desks) pay per portfolio or per MWh. They get lower imbalance cost, timely nominations, position and margin visibility per 15 minutes, and an option to outsource balance responsibility entirely.
- **Large consumers and producers** (BASF, USG Chemelot, wind and solar producers) use consumption analysis, forecast management and customer-trading self-service through their supplier's platform, paying through their supply contract.
- **End customers** (over 7 million households and 3 million business customers of Dutch supplier clients) use the white-label portal for free; the supplier pays.
- **The strategic asset** is the integrated market-operations data model: every connection, contract, meter reading, allocation volume, nomination, trade, invoice and payment lives in one place across 20 million connections processed daily for 50+ energy companies, which makes new propositions, portals, steering signals and managed services compound rather than bolt on.

## Strategic spine (1/3/5-year horizons)

- **Year 1 — One platform, one flow.** Consolidate Ecedo, Energy21, Jules, Gridhub and Nemon customers onto one identity, data and integration foundation; ship the Allocation 2.0 and 15-minute market changes across every tenant; make the proposition builder and white-label portal standard attach; run BRP as a Service at scale; land the Nemon integration and the first cross-sold Iberian modules.
- **Year 3 — The operations standard for Benelux and Iberia, a foothold in Germany, Belgium and the UK.** Modular suites with a certified-integration catalogue, market-communication adapters per country, managed services (BRP, market entry, licensing) as a growth engine, and selective acquisitions that add markets or capabilities.
- **Year 5 — European scale and autonomous operations.** Multi-country operation on one platform, AI-assisted operations (billing-exception triage, forecast tuning, steering, customer-service resolution) under human supervision, and data products (consumption validation, market benchmarks, flexibility value) that make the platform the operating system of the European energy chain.
