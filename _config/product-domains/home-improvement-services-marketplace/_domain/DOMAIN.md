# Home Improvement Services Marketplace

This domain models the multi-sided marketplace operated by Solvari: homeowners describe renovation, maintenance, or residential-sustainability projects; Solvari qualifies and routes that demand to suitable home-improvement specialists; professionals pay for subscriptions, credits, campaigns, and related platform services; and affiliate publishers can contribute qualified demand.

## Why this boundary

Solvari describes itself as a platform that connects homeowners with vetted specialists in the home-improvement market. Its public homeowner journey moves from project description to contact with specialists and selection of the preferred company. Its professional proposition combines a selectable lead marketplace, automated campaigns, company profiles, CRM and API functionality, packages, credits, and account-management support. Vortex Capital Partners describes Solvari as a leading Benelux platform with particular strength in sustainability-led categories and a recurring-revenue model representing more than 60% of revenue.

This makes **Home Improvement Services Marketplace** the most useful product-domain boundary. It is broader than a quote form or lead-generation website, but narrower than all residential construction, energy technology, or Solvari's corporate acquisition portfolio.

## Core scope

- homeowner education, project scoping, request submission, comparison, communication, and feedback
- specialist onboarding, identity and business verification, profiles, service areas, capacity preferences, lead selection, campaigns, CRM workflow, and performance coaching
- request qualification, regional and category matching, ranking, lead pricing, allocation, marketplace quality, reviews, complaints, and lead claims
- subscriptions, credit bundles, entitlements, invoicing, and collections for professional customers
- affiliate attribution, embedded forms, API integrations, consent, and partner settlement
- multi-brand and multi-country migration needed to absorb acquired marketplaces without losing customer, professional, or attribution continuity

## Adjacent scope

- renovation and sustainability advice that helps a homeowner form a project before requesting specialists
- subsidy and eligibility guidance that improves project readiness, while official authorities remain the source of truth
- professional sales enablement, lightweight CRM, and APIs that improve lead conversion
- analytics and AI-assisted request enrichment, matching, fraud detection, and operational decision support

## Explicitly excluded

- performing, warranting, financing, or insuring the underlying construction work
- manufacturing or selling solar panels, heat pumps, insulation, windows, or other building products
- acting as the contractor of record or party to the homeowner-professional service agreement
- full construction ERP, field-service management, payroll, or materials procurement for specialists
- official subsidy approval, building permits, certification, or regulatory inspection
- general B2B service sourcing unrelated to improving, renovating, maintaining, or making homes more sustainable

## Value exchange

- **Homeowners** receive free, low-friction access to relevant professionals, comparison context, reputation signals, and guidance for making a consequential choice.
- **Home-improvement specialists** pay for controlled access to relevant project demand and for software or support that helps turn that demand into profitable work.
- **Affiliate and content partners** contribute attributable demand and receive agreed compensation for accepted leads.
- **Marketplace operators** protect request quality, specialist trust, fair allocation, regulatory compliance, and recovery when a match or lead is disputed.
- **The marketplace** earns recurring package revenue and usage-linked credit or lead revenue while compounding better matching, reputation, and market-coverage data.

## Strategic vision

Make every serious home-improvement project easier to start with confidence and every reputable specialist better able to grow from demand that fits its craft, region, and available capacity.

## Strategic sequence

1. **Year 1 — trusted qualified core:** improve request completeness, contactability, match relevance, specialist verification, transparent lead economics, and issue recovery.
2. **Year 3 — adaptive growth platform:** personalize project guidance and allocation, embed professional CRM and APIs, make capacity-aware campaigns more effective, and unify acquired brands and countries on shared controls.
3. **Year 5 — home-improvement decision network:** use consented property, project, reputation, and outcome signals to coordinate the path from early intent through trusted professional selection across European markets.
