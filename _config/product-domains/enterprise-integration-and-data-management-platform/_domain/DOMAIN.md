# Enterprise Integration & Data Management Platform

Updated: 2026-09-05

## Domain thesis

This domain models the product and operating landscape of Enable U as an enterprise integration, API, data-management and managed-services platform. The factual core is grounded in Enable U's public product pages and the Vortex Capital Partners investment profile. The customer strategies, KPI baselines and targets, product-brick boundaries, deployment topology, data ownership and team design are analytical models created for Productscape; they are not presented as Enable U's internal plans, architecture, financials or organization chart.

Enable U describes itself as **data & integration enablers** and reports more than 180 specialists and more than 400 customers. Vortex records a 2020 buyout and describes a digital-integration solution provider focused on growing its internally developed platform through organic growth and acquisitions; the investment profile cites more than 300 Dutch municipalities and more than 5,000 secure integrations. Enable U's published history starts in 2005 and records JNET joining in 2021, Scamander in 2024 and Estreme in 2025.

## Customer value

The platform serves seven modeled roles across three systems of value:

1. CIOs and architects turn a fragmented, risky integration estate into an owned roadmap and executable guardrails.
2. Developers and reliability managers deliver and operate reusable integrations with business-chain traceability and bounded recovery.
3. Data leaders, finance operations and ISV partners consume outcome products: governed data, authoritative distribution, Peppol, e-VOI and reusable government connectivity.

The strategic north star is not the number of messages transported. It is the share of critical customer outcomes that complete reliably through a governed, reusable chain and can migrate or change without losing meaning, trust or evidence.

## Product portfolio

- **Managed Integrations** combines build and run responsibility around predictable service levels.
- **Easy Integration** standardizes Azure Integration Services with security, traceability and phased migration.
- **Boomi iPaaS and services** supports low-code hybrid integration with implementation and lifecycle expertise.
- **OpenTunnel and Digikoppeling** concentrate Dutch-government protocols and national-facility connectivity.
- **API Gateway and Digital Identity** concentrate API policy, developer lifecycle and DigiD/eHerkenning/eIDAS adapters.
- **GGM Data Management Platform** combines a reusable data foundation, governance, models and analytics with customer data ownership.
- **Data Distribution** manages authoritative register subscriptions and recipient delivery.
- **Trusted Business Transactions** packages Peppol and e-VOI around straight-through finance and payroll outcomes.
- **Managed Services and Consultancy** move from maturity scan and architecture through implementation to recurring operation.

## Architecture logic

The modeled architecture separates reusable meaning from runtime choice:

- Integration definitions, mappings and contracts are independent of Azure, Boomi, OpenTunnel and Layer7 adapters.
- Tenant, product, deployment, owner, service objective, certificate and supplier dependency share one control plane.
- Government and identity profiles version behind stable application-facing contracts.
- Message execution stores correlation, checkpoints and outcome state without creating an unrestricted payload lake.
- Governed data products carry ownership, lineage, purpose and quality from source through dashboard or API.
- Operations recovers bounded cohorts based on idempotency and downstream reconciliation, with human approval for irreversible legal, payroll, finance or privacy-sensitive actions.

## Commercial and organizational logic

Vortex's investment thesis emphasizes recurring revenue, scalability and European specialist growth through organic development and acquisitions. The modeled product strategy therefore treats the reusable catalog, shared control plane, managed service tiers and attach of data, API and transaction products as economic capabilities. Four groups and twelve teams cover approximately 120 modeled product, delivery, operations and customer-enablement roles; the rest of a 180+ person company is intentionally outside this domain topology. All headcount, ownership and KPI values are modeled seeds.
