# Occupational Health and Safety Platform

This product domain models the business operated by **Kitry** (kitry.com; vortexcp.com/investment/kitry/): a Belgian software vendor with more than 35 years of expertise in occupational health and safety software (the company traces its specialisation to 1989 and its current legal entity, Kitry SA in Nivelles, to 2003). Kitry sells a **modular, multilingual SaaS platform** for occupational health, workplace safety, work accidents and occupational diseases, employee and manager portals and cross-module analytics, hosted on HDS-certified (Hébergeur de Données de Santé) and ISO 27001-certified infrastructure inside the EEA, together with a services model that runs functional assessment, configuration, project management, data migration, training, change management and ongoing support for every customer. Kitry serves large, demanding organisations in France, Belgium, Luxembourg and the Netherlands (named references include SNCF, Thales, Ville de Paris, Ville de Bruxelles, Umicore, Empreva, Engie, BNP, EIB, Stellantis, EDF, RATP, CNRS, Disney and BASF), with 150+ customer organisations, 6,000+ active users and more than 4 million employee records tracked. **Vortex Capital Partners**, a Dutch private-equity investor in software SMEs, became a strategic partner in a buyout announced on 29 March 2022 and backs commercial expansion, international rollout and the "Kitry as a Service" pure-play SaaS transition. It is an instructive example of **regulated vertical SaaS**: health data under medical confidentiality, country-specific labour-law obligations that change every year, self-service adoption by every employee of a large organisation, and a professional services arm that operates the same product.

## Domain boundary

**Core scope.** Everything an employer's occupational health service, prevention function and HR department need to run the lifecycle *Know the workforce → Plan surveillance → Examine and advise → Keep the medical file → Assess and control risks → Handle events → Serve employees and managers → Prove compliance* on one platform, following Kitry's module set:

- **Workforce and exposure data** — workers, contracts, assignments, sites and workstations synchronised from HR systems; exposure and risk profiles per workstation (special surveillance categories such as night work, chemical, biological and physical exposures, safety-critical posts); surveillance rules that derive the required examination type and periodicity per country and per profile.
- **Occupational health** — convocation planning and appointment scheduling with SMS and e-mail reminders, pre-visit questionnaires, consultation workspace for physicians and nurses, capture from connected measurement devices (audiometry, spirometry, vision, ECG, biometrics), fitness-for-work opinions with restrictions and accommodations, vaccination and health-promotion campaigns, secure integrated teleconsultation, and a complete occupational medical file (DMST in France, medical dossier in Belgium) with strict medical confidentiality, access segregation and transfer between services.
- **Workplace safety** — risk assessment per workstation and site (DUERP in France, global prevention plan and annual action plan in Belgium), hazardous situation reporting from the field, inspections, audits and compliance checks, action plans followed to closure, and a chemical product inventory with safety data sheets, hazard classification and exposure tracking; support for ISO 45001 management systems.
- **Work accidents and occupational diseases** — event declaration with witnesses, injuries and circumstances, occupational disease cases, country-specific national forms and connectors (French CERFA/Ameli declarations, Belgian Fedris and insurer declarations), root-cause analysis, frequency and severity statistics, and follow-up of absence, return-to-work and recognition.
- **Portals** — an employee portal (appointments, questionnaires, documents, teleconsultation, hazardous situation reports) and a manager portal (team fitness status, restrictions, actions, declarations) with role-based access that never exposes medical content.
- **Analytics and reporting** — cross-module dashboards and KPIs, regulatory and annual activity reports (annual report of the occupational health service, Belgian annual prevention report, social and safety indicators), collective health monitoring and exposure epidemiology.
- **Foundation** — identity, SSO, roles and consent; a configuration engine for forms, workflows, roles and business rules ("configurable to your needs" and sector pre-configurations); country regulation content for France, Belgium, Luxembourg and the Netherlands; audit trail and retention; shared or dedicated HDS/ISO 27001 SaaS hosting with no access from outside the EEA; French, Dutch and English interfaces; ecosystem integrations for electronic signature (Goodflag), telehealth (Vonage), SMS (Cegedim) and measurement devices (Eolys, FIM, Essilor, JLM Medical, Siemens).
- **Services** — a four-phase deployment (functional assessment, configuration, project management, training), proven data migration from legacy systems, change management, reactive support and dedicated account managers.

**Adjacent scope (modeled lightly).** Environment and ESG reporting, general quality management, HR core (payroll, absence management) and the clinical activities of general healthcare are out of the product; the platform exchanges data with HRIS, insurers and national bodies rather than replacing them. External prevention services (Belgian SEPP/EDPB such as IDEWE or Mensura, French SPSTI) are modeled as substitutes and partners, not as target customers: Kitry's core buyer is the organisation with its own internal or autonomous occupational health service.

**Explicitly excluded.** Kitry is not an electronic health record for treatment, not a payroll or HR suite, not an insurer claims system, and not a generic EHS-plus-ESG enterprise suite. This distinguishes the domain from `digital-medication-management` (clinical pharmacy) and `mental-wellbeing-community-platform` (consumer wellbeing). Here the business is annual SaaS subscriptions per module and per workforce size, hosting tier (shared or dedicated HDS SaaS), and professional services for deployment, migration and training.

## Value exchange

- **Occupational health services** of large employers (physicians, nurses, service coordinators) get a certified, confidential system of record that plans every required examination, removes administrative friction from the consultation day and proves the service's activity to the employer and the authorities.
- **HSE and prevention teams** get one living risk assessment linked to the same workstations and exposures the physician sees, hazardous situations that arrive from the floor, and action plans that close.
- **HR administrators and managers** keep employee data synchronised so surveillance is triggered automatically, declare accidents correctly and on time, and see fitness clearance and restrictions for their teams without seeing medical content.
- **Employees** book and manage their own visits, complete questionnaires privately, hold their documents and raise safety concerns; more than half of workers in Kitry's French, Belgian and Luxembourg customers use self-service features.
- **Kitry's own consultants** deploy, migrate, train and support customers on the same platform and keep the configuration current with regulatory change.
- **The strategic asset** is the integrated health-and-safety data model, in which a worker, a workstation, its exposures, the surveillance rules, the medical file, the risks, the events and the actions are one connected record, hosted under HDS/ISO 27001 with 35 years of regulatory know-how for France, Belgium, Luxembourg and the Netherlands. The installed base of blue-chip and public-sector customers and the migration expertise that brings decades of medical files across are the second asset; portals, teleconsultation and analytics compound on both.

## Strategic spine (1/3/5-year horizons)

- **Year 1 — Pure-play SaaS on a proven product.** Under Vortex, strengthen leadership and the organisation, complete the "Kitry as a Service" transition (shared and dedicated HDS SaaS, standard release train), lift self-service adoption above half of the workforce at every customer, ship the manager portal and teleconsultation as standard, and keep FR/BE/LU/NL regulatory content current (DMST decree, prevention passport, Fedris and insurer forms).
- **Year 3 — The reference platform for internal occupational health and safety services in Benelux and France.** Standard HRIS and SSO connectors, device and telehealth integrations out of the box, ISO 45001-ready safety module, analytics that make surveillance compliance and accident rates steerable, sector pre-configurations (industry, chemicals and pharma, healthcare, universities, public sector, finance, transport, services), the Netherlands as a full market, and selective acquisitions that add geography or adjacent capability.
- **Year 5 — Continuous, data-driven prevention at European scale.** Workforce, exposure and health data that update as HR and field events flow in, assisted drafting of fitness opinions and risk assessments under professional supervision, interoperable medical files across services and countries, predictive collective health monitoring, and a partner ecosystem of devices, telehealth and external prevention services that makes the platform the operating system of workplace prevention.
