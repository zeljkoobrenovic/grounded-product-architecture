# BI Dashboard Extensions Platform

This product domain models the business operated by **Infotopics | Apps for Tableau** (appsfortableau.infotopics.com, vortexcp.com/investment/infotopics-apps-for-tableau/): a Hardenberg (NL) and London based software company that builds premium extensions for business-intelligence platforms. The company grew out of Infotopics, a Tableau Gold Partner since 2011; when Tableau released dashboard extensions in July 2018 (version 2018.2) the Apps for Tableau team built its first product, ShowMeMore, which was demonstrated in the Tableau Conference 2018 keynote, and the team won the Tableau Innovative Solution Award that year. Founded in 2018 by Merlijn Buit (CTO) and Richard van Wijk (CEO), the company today serves 600+ organisations in 150+ countries, ships 30+ extensions, works with 32 partner firms, employs about 30 people and counts two of the 25 global Tableau Hall of Fame Visionaries on its team. On 26 March 2026 Vortex Capital Partners completed a buyout to fund international growth, portfolio expansion with AI functionality and selective acquisitions. It is an instructive example of **an ecosystem software business**: the product lives inside somebody else's platform (Tableau Cloud, Tableau Server, Tableau Desktop, and since 2022 Power BI and Looker Studio), so runtime, distribution, licensing, security and platform-vendor risk are as strategic as the features themselves.

## Domain boundary

**Core scope.** Everything needed to turn a static BI dashboard into an interactive decision-making tool, and to run the vendor that sells that capability, along the lifecycle *Discover → Trial → Build → Use → Distribute → Deploy → Govern → Renew*:

- **Write-back and collaboration** — WriteBackExtreme (governed write-back from Tableau to the customer's own databases with audit trail, rollback, role management, SSO, one-click Windows and Linux installers, no internet required), InputTables (Excel-like cell-level input, comments and dynamic columns inside a SuperTable) and FinanceTables (P&Ls, budget reports and management statements). Use cases: planning and forecasting, what-if scenarios, data correction, surveys, annotations, task lists and approvals.
- **Self-service tables and discovery** — SuperTables (Excel-like grid with pivot, grouping, conditional formatting, formulas, Excel export, personalised saved views, server-side row model for millions of rows, generative fill), DrillDownTree, HierarchyFilter and ProcessMining.
- **Visual storytelling and insight delivery** — ShowMeMore chart types, PowerKPIs as central KPI entry point, PictureThis image tables, VizSlides presentation export, MarginalHistogram and EasyDesigns.
- **Distribution, guidance and adoption** — MailScheduler (scheduled, per-recipient filtered delivery of PDF, PNG, CSV and Excel to email, Microsoft Teams, Slack and network drives with placeholders, API and audit trail; reaches non-Tableau users), DashboardGuide onboarding overlays, DashboardUsage analytics and TrexReplacer for extension URL management across workbooks.
- **Extension runtime and platform** — the shared extension framework on the Tableau Extensions API and Viz Extensions, the dashboard data connector, rendering and export services, messaging gateways, write-back data stores and connectors, AI services, and the adapters that carry the same products to Power BI (SuperTables, PictureThis, InputTables, WriteBackExtreme) and Looker Studio.
- **Deployment, licensing and trust** — the SaaS hosting model (Infotopics hosts the extension web apps and license service; extensions are safelisted in Tableau) versus the Enterprise self-hosted model (ExtensionsManager installer and admin console, license keys and updates through the Enterprise Customer Portal, on-premises or private cloud without internet); usage metering by anonymous unique tokens over a rolling 12-month window covering creators, explorers and viewers; Free (1–5 users, all extensions, no time limit), Professional (up to 100 users, cloud, annual billing) and Enterprise (unlimited, SaaS or on-premises, dedicated customer success manager, priority support, purchase orders) plans; WriteBackExtreme and MailScheduler as Enterprise-only products; ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, annual penetration testing and SLAs.
- **Go-to-market and customer success** — website, gallery and demo workbooks, self-service signup and trials, the Tableau Exchange and Microsoft AppSource listings, the 32-firm consulting and reseller partner network (Slalom, Biztory, USEReady, Big X Data among them), documentation at docs.infotopics.com, webinars, student licences and the support portal.

**Adjacent scope (modeled lightly).** AI-driven functionality on top of the extensions (generative fill, insight narration, AI-assisted configuration), the consulting services heritage of Infotopics, and post-acquisition integration of add-on companies under the Vortex buy-and-build thesis.

**Explicitly excluded.** The vendor does not build the BI platform itself, does not host or own customer data warehouses, does not run a general consulting practice as its business, and is not a marketplace. This distinguishes the domain from `cloud-data-and-ai-platform` (the data platform itself) and `internal-developer-platform`. Here the business is software subscriptions per organisation, priced by active dashboard users and deployment model, sold directly and through partners.

## Value exchange

- **Dashboard creators and BI platform leads** at 600+ organisations (Bank of America, Pfizer, Siemens, Johnson & Johnson, Amgen, BNP Paribas, Sony Interactive Entertainment, McDonald's, Toyota, US Bank, Post Holdings) pay an annual subscription that scales with active users and deployment model. They get dashboards that Excel-minded users adopt (a Post Holdings senior director with 1,000+ Tableau users: "SuperTables has made Tableau so much more accessible to people that are used to Excel"), fewer bespoke dashboards to build, and capabilities the platform lacks natively.
- **Business users, planners and executives** use the extensions for free within their organisation's licence; they get self-service tables, governed write-back for forecasts and comments (Pfizer: "Everybody is using the dashboard made with WriteBackExtreme because it's straightforward and intuitive"), and reports delivered where they work.
- **IT administrators and security reviewers** get an on-premises option, SSO, role management, audit trails, ISO 27001 evidence and penetration-test reports, in exchange for owning hosting, upgrades and network access.
- **Partners** earn consulting and resale margin and get demo licences, enablement and deal support; the vendor gains local-language reach in 150+ countries with a 30-person team.
- **The strategic asset** is the extension runtime plus the installed base: one shared framework, data connector, licensing and deployment stack across 30+ extensions and three BI platforms, so every new extension, AI feature or platform port compounds on existing distribution and trust.

## Strategic spine (1/3/5-year horizons)

- **Year 1 — Enterprise depth and AI inside the extensions.** Ship AI functionality where customers already are (generative fill, narrative insights, AI-assisted extension configuration and write-back validation), harden the Enterprise deployment path (one-click installers, ExtensionsManager, TrexReplacer, upgrade automation), grow the partner-sourced pipeline and professionalise sales and customer success under Vortex ownership.
- **Year 3 — The multi-platform extensions standard.** Feature parity of the core products (SuperTables, WriteBackExtreme, InputTables, MailScheduler, PowerKPIs) across Tableau, Power BI and Looker Studio on one runtime and one licence; a governed AI layer for planning and decision workflows; international expansion in North America, the UK and DACH through partners; one or two selective acquisitions that add extensions, platforms or geographies.
- **Year 5 — The decision layer on top of any BI platform.** Extensions become a platform-independent decision and planning layer (write-back, workflow, distribution, guidance) with AI agents that draft plans, explain variances and route approvals under human control, licensed per organisation regardless of BI vendor, with the customer's data never leaving their own environment.
