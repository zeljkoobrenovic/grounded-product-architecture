# Transfer Pricing Compliance Platform

This product domain models the business operated by **Reptune** (reptune.tax, formerly TP Tuned; vortexcp.com/investment/reptune/): an Amsterdam-based tax-technology company founded in 2015 by transfer pricing specialists with Big 4 and in-house experience (Lennart van den Kommer and Allard Posthuma, later joined by David Zářecký). Reptune sells **Reptune.net**, a proprietary SaaS platform that automates three-tiered transfer pricing documentation (Master File, Local Files, Country-by-Country Reporting) for more than 170 countries, together with a flexible service model that ranges from a client-led licence to Reptune-led full-service preparation at fixed rates per Local File. Vortex Capital Partners became majority shareholder alongside management in a buyout announced on 5 August 2024 and backs international expansion, organically and through acquisitions. It is an instructive example of **vertical compliance SaaS built by domain experts**: a structured data model that turns regulatory documentation into a generated artefact, a knowledge base of country rules that has to stay current, and a services arm that operates the same product.

## Domain boundary

**Core scope.** Everything a multinational tax team needs to run the annual lifecycle *Model → Scope → Collect → Generate → Localise → File → Monitor → Defend* on one platform, following Reptune.net's feature set:

- **Transfer pricing model and data** — legal entities, company types, functional analyses, transfer pricing methods and benchmarks configured once and applied across all entities and reports; the register of in-scope intercompany transactions per entity; segmented financials and P&L segmentation per activity; bulk data entry through Excel templates, data exchange and validation, and direct ERP or operational transfer pricing integrations.
- **Automated report generator** — Master Files and Local Files at the press of a button, built on an OECD-aligned template structure, with best-practice standard templates, localised templates (for example Italy), custom templates, custom text functionality, report styling in the client's corporate identity, draft or final output, built-in validation that flags gaps before generation, and archived benchmarks and intercompany agreements inserted automatically.
- **Localisation and translation** — local content, format, filing and language requirements for 170+ countries maintained by transfer pricing specialists, AI-powered machine translation into 28 languages with transfer pricing glossaries, human-certified translation on request, and support with local forms and procedures.
- **Country-by-Country Reporting** — CbCR data management with manual or bulk entry, automated validation and an Actions Required page, filing-ready XML and Excel output, EU Public CbCR in XHTML with inline XBRL generated from existing CbCR data, CbCR notifications, and upcoming Australian Public CbCR.
- **Compliance workflow and dashboard** — scoping of documentation per entity and country, task assignment, collaboration and e-mail reminders, automatically calculated deadlines for Master File, Local Files, CbCR and CbCR notifications in every country, and a dashboard with widgets, graphs and a world map that shows real-time compliance status and financial performance across jurisdictions.
- **Benchmarks** — benchmark studies and arm's-length ranges archived in the platform and compared against each entity's segmented actual performance.
- **Legal module** — a single source of truth for legal entities, shareholders, directors, authorised signatories, intercompany agreements and corporate documents across jurisdictions, time-stamped corporate data with history, current and historical legal structure charts, and DocuSign integration to generate, send, store and track e-signatures.
- **AI capabilities** — R-AI, the platform's own assistant for onboarding and product questions in any language with a coming Local Requirements country-expert mode; AI-assisted writing and editing inside every text field; AI-powered machine translation.
- **Foundation** — unlimited users, SSO, custom user profiles and access rights (read-only, location-restricted), divisions for multi-group structures, ISO 27001-certified security, document rendering and export, audit trail and versioning, and a free two-week trial with go-live in days without installation.
- **Services** — three service levels on the same platform (On Demand client-led, Collaborative joint effort, Full Service Reptune-led) with 2, 4 or 8 included expert hours per plan; transfer pricing consultation covering value chain analysis, CbCR analysis, functional analysis, model review, policy write-up, controversy, business restructurings, Advance Pricing Agreements and Mutual Agreement Procedures; risk management and operational transfer pricing support.

**Adjacent scope (modeled lightly).** Operational transfer pricing (intercompany price setting and true-ups) through partner integrations rather than native pricing; Pillar Two and CbCR safe-harbour analysis; controversy and APA support as advisory services rather than product.

**Explicitly excluded.** Reptune is not a tax authority filing portal, not a general tax-provision or corporate income tax engine, not a statutory accounting or consolidation system, and not an ERP. Intercompany invoicing and settlement, treasury and intercompany financing pricing, indirect tax and customs valuation, and Pillar Two GloBE calculations are outside the platform. This distinguishes the domain from `payments-and-revenue-infrastructure` (money movement) and `enterprise-architecture-management-platform` (enterprise modeling). Here the business is annual SaaS subscriptions by plan (Core up to 25 Local Files, Advanced up to 50, Enterprise 50+), add-ons (machine translation, CbCR, SSO, R-AI Country Expert, custom templates, custom transactional data model, custom development), fixed-rate documentation services per Local File, and advisory hours.

## Value exchange

- **In-house tax teams of multinationals** with 5 to 500+ entities (named customers include TomTom, Stahl, Avnet, Ravago and Stanley Black & Decker; more than 150 multinationals worldwide) pay an annual subscription that scales with the number of Local Files, plus add-ons and optional services. They get complete, consistent and locally compliant documentation with 50 to 75 percent less time and cost, deadlines that are never missed, documentation that has "saved the day" in tax audits, and a transparent no-surprise fee structure.
- **Corporate legal counsel** gets a shared legal-entity and intercompany-agreement system of record with structure charts and e-signatures that feeds the transfer pricing documentation directly.
- **Tax advisory firms** (international and local) license the platform to prepare documentation for their own clients at a fraction of the traditional effort.
- **Reptune's own specialists** operate the same platform for full-service and collaborative clients, preparing documentation in about 25 percent of the time of traditional advisers; clients can switch between service levels at any time because the platform is already set up for them.
- **The strategic asset** is the intelligent transfer pricing data model, developed since 2015, in which entities, transactions, functional profiles, methods, benchmarks, agreements, financials and country rules are structured so that Local Files, Master Files and CbCR are generated without manual changes to the exported files. The country regulations knowledge base for 170+ countries and the specialist team that maintains it are the second asset; AI assistance, translation, dashboards and the legal module compound on both.

## Strategic spine (1/3/5-year horizons)

- **Year 1 — Commercial scale on a proven product.** Under Vortex, strengthen the organisation, sales development and go-to-market, ship the R-AI country-expert mode, EU Public CbCR and Australian Public CbCR, deepen the legal module (DocuSign, structure charts), and keep the 170-country regulation base current while onboarding new multinationals in days.
- **Year 3 — The central transfer pricing platform for European multinationals and their advisers.** Direct ERP and operational transfer pricing integrations as standard, a partner channel for advisory firms, benchmarks and risk analytics that make audits predictable, multi-language documentation as a default, and selective acquisitions that add geography, content or adjacent compliance capability.
- **Year 5 — Continuous, AI-assisted transfer pricing compliance at global scale.** Documentation that updates as transactions and financials flow in, agentic drafting and review under specialist supervision, controversy-ready evidence packs, and data products (regulation intelligence, benchmark libraries, audit-outcome insight) that make the platform the trade's operating system across regions.
