# Solvari Home Improvement Marketplace

Research checked: 2026-09-06.

Solvari connects home-improvement demand with professional supply. This domain models the decision and commercial relationship from an uncertain home project to a useful professional conversation, supported by recurring professional participation. Renovation and energy-saving categories share the marketplace, while differing in qualification, regional coverage, and the evidence a homeowner needs.

This is a separate company-specific domain requested alongside the existing `home-improvement-services-marketplace`. It emphasizes Joost-assisted project clarification, the difference between the professional buyer and daily lead coordinator, contract-versioned lead economics, and evidence limitations. Neither domain is a verified account of Solvari's internal systems.

## Boundary and value exchange

- **Homeowners** seek a clear brief, relevant local choices, controlled contact, and credible selection evidence. The advertised quote-comparison flow is free and non-binding.
- **Professional business owners** buy platform participation and demand access, deciding whether acquisition costs and package commitments support profitable capacity utilization.
- **Professional lead coordinators** work the incoming opportunities, record follow-up, and return usable conversion evidence. This is an inferred role distinct from the person who approves subscription spend; small firms may combine both roles.
- **Affiliate publishers** contribute attributable project demand through forms or API connections and receive agreed compensation.
- **Marketplace quality operators** review eligibility, lead disputes, data-use exceptions, and supply gaps. Their modeled mission protects marketplace usefulness alongside commercial growth.

Core scope includes category guidance and Joost, project intake, request quality, professional verification and profiles, matching and contact allocation, lead delivery, campaigns, package entitlements and credits, CRM follow-up, subscriptions, affiliate attribution and settlement, reviews, and human exception handling. Shared identity, communications, measurement, reliability, and brand integration enable these flows.

Adjacent scope includes permission-aware property history, partner CRM connections, and reusable country/category policies. New-country expansion is a proposed horizon, not a promise or inference that every group brand shares the same offer.

Excluded scope: execution of building work, guaranteed installation quality, homeowner project-payment escrow, construction ERP, materials procurement, financing decisions, subsidy approval, and autonomous selection of a contractor. Professional subscription billing and affiliate settlement are in scope; the homeowner's work contract is directly with the specialist.

## Strategic thesis

Make it easier for a homeowner to reach a useful, well-informed specialist conversation and for a reputable professional to justify continued participation from evidence of relevant demand and controlled acquisition cost.

The proposed domain north star is **Qualified requests with a useful professional conversation within seven days**. Count distinct eligible submitted requests in the denominator, and count a request once when at least one permitted recipient has a two-way conversation that the homeowner confirms as relevant. Publish outcome-observation coverage beside this rate. An application, allocated lead, reply, quote, contracted job, completed job, and verified experience remain different events.

Guardrails include unwanted-contact reports, no-match disclosure, eligibility decisions overturned on review, professional budget overshoot, unresolved credit disputes, and outcome-observation coverage. Premium commercial settings must be explainable; paying for a package does not substitute for relevance or trust checks.

## Proposed horizons

1. **Year 1 — prove the match and the economics.** Establish a dated baseline; make Joost's brief editable; explain contact allocation and no-match outcomes; connect lead charges to delivery and correction records; close the loop from professional follow-up to useful homeowner contact.
2. **Year 3 — retain through workflow value.** Improve capacity-aware campaigns and permission-aware property context; make CRM synchronization and affiliate reconciliation reliable; evaluate assistant quality by category; measure renewal against cohort value, not only purchased volume.
3. **Year 5 — scale a governable marketplace.** Reuse country and acquired-brand capabilities with contract, consent, identity, and metric lineage intact; preserve local category differences; expand only where supply quality and contribution economics support the customer promise.

These are Productscape proposals relative to adoption of this model, not Solvari's published roadmap. KPI baselines are **Not measured**. Any numeric pilot targets in the model are explicitly proposed acceptance criteria, never claims about present performance.

## Evidence and assumptions

Start with [Solvari](https://www.solvari.nl/) and [Vortex's investment page](https://vortexcp.com/investment/solvari/), then use the dated source register in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). Vortex lists a 2018 buyout and describes a shift toward recurring revenue. Public scale counters represent company-reported scope; they are not measured funnels for this model.

The architecture, modules, stores, ownership, staff allocations, internal console, and detailed operational roles are design hypotheses. Published references establish capability context, not implementation proof. Joost's existence is public; its model provider, prompts, evaluation pipeline, and production control architecture are not disclosed by the inspected sources.

Professional pricing varies by package, contract, and lead settings. The inspected pricing page has ambiguous Starter details and a credit-bundle arithmetic inconsistency. This model therefore records entitlement and contract versions without encoding a universal live tariff. Vortex mentions five strategic acquisitions while Solvari's April 2024 Offerte.nl announcement calls it the sixth acquisition in three years; the definitions are not reconciled. A historical revenue forecast is not treated as realized revenue.
