## Discrepancies and interpretation choices

- Vortex describes five supported strategic acquisitions, while the April 2024 announcement calls Offerte.nl the sixth acquisition in three years. The definitions and time windows are not reconciled; neither is converted into a definitive current group acquisition count. [Vortex](https://vortexcp.com/investment/solvari/), [Solvari announcement](https://www.solvari.nl/blog/overname-offertenl).
- Starter package presentation is ambiguous, and the credit-bundle table contains a percentage/arithmetic mismatch. The architecture therefore models effective order-form terms and versioned entitlements. It does not reproduce those values as a pricing policy. [Solvari pricing](https://www.solvari.nl/pro/pricing).
- Applications, matched requests, delivered leads, quotes, contracted work, completed work, and reviewed experiences are different measures. The proposed north star depends on observed two-way contact and always needs an outcome-observation coverage measure. This metric design is an inference from the public flow, not a reported Solvari measure. [Solvari](https://www.solvari.nl/).
- A country team role or an acquired brand's footprint does not prove that every Solvari product, price, or support process is available in that country. The modeled operating focus is the Netherlands and Belgium; later expansion remains a proposed horizon. [Vortex](https://vortexcp.com/investment/solvari/), [Solvari acquisition context](https://www.solvari.nl/blog/overname-offertenl).

## Competition research

The eight players in `business/competition.json` use official sources, retain the reported metric scope, and separate direct matching alternatives from transactional substitutes and an international benchmark:

- [Werkspot](https://www.werkspot.nl/over-ons), [Homedeal](https://www.homedeal.nl/ons-verhaal/), [Trustoo](https://trustoo.nl/over-trustoo/), and [Slimster](https://slimster.nl/over/) establish Dutch discovery and quote alternatives.
- [Trustup](https://www.trustup.be/nl) establishes the regional renovation alternative; [investor context](https://investsud.be/trustup-leve-5me-pour-assurer-sa-croissance/20250116_cp_trustup-leve-5-millions/) supports its location. The homepage professional counter does not establish active paid supply.
- [Zoofy](https://zoofy.nl/over-ons/) and [Ring Twice](https://ringtwice.be/nl/about) cover different task-booking/provider models. Their cumulative customer/community counters are not Solvari-comparable annual project volumes.
- [Checkatrade](https://www.checkatrade.com/) provides a UK trust and professional-platform benchmark. Its precise headquarters was not established in the inspected page and is explicitly left unverified.

Offerte.nl, Buldit, Offrea, and Casius are treated as acquired-group context rather than independent competitors. Word of mouth and a professional's own direct channels remain substitutes without invented corporate metrics. No market shares are calculated from these sources.
