# Household Finance Brokerage Platform

This product domain models the business operated by **Peasy** (peasy.be; vortexcp.com/investment/peasy/): a Belgian "phygital" brokerage and online comparison platform for household finances headquartered in Zaventem. Peasy grew out of **hypotheek.winkel**, a mortgage brokerage chain started in 2001 that today runs about 70 franchised offices across Flanders and Brussels and compares the conditions of 25 banks daily, free of charge for the buyer because the chosen bank pays a referral commission. Vortex Capital Partners acquired a majority stake in hypotheek.winkel in 2020 (then 68 branches) and, together with management, formed Horizon Group, which acquired **Verzekeringen.be / Assurances.be** (2021) and **Aanbieders.be / Mesfournisseurs.be** (2022) to add insurance, energy and telecom comparison. Early 2025 the group rebranded as Peasy, a single consumer front door that promises "besparen zonder gedoe" across mortgage loans, insurance and energy under one roof. Peasy reports more than 150 experts, 70 offices, more than 50,000 contracts concluded per year and more than 50 partners; customer care operates from Hasselt and Oujda (Morocco); the group employs roughly 200 people and generates revenues of around €30 million. Vortex highlights an in-house training academy educating about 30 advisors per year and the decision to keep investing through a downturn in which the Belgian mortgage market contracted by roughly 40 percent. In December 2025 **Meilleurtaux** (France; backed by Silver Lake; in Belgium since the 2023 acquisition of MiD Finance, mainly in Wallonia) agreed to acquire 100 percent of Peasy, creating a national platform with 81 offices; Peasy CEO **David Geerts** leads the combined Belgian and Luxembourg operations. The domain is an instructive example of **omnichannel brokerage**: digital comparison and simulation tools generate demand, human experts convert complex, high-value decisions such as a mortgage, and a partner-paid commission model keeps the service free for the consumer.

## Domain boundary

**Core scope.** Everything a Belgian household needs to compare, choose, close and keep optimising its recurring financial contracts, following the Peasy proposition:

- **Consumer comparison platform** (peasy.be, verzekeringen.be, aanbieders.be; Dutch and French) — mortgage simulators (how much can I borrow, monthly repayment, feasibility check, notary costs, financial plan, refinancing), insurance comparison for car, fire, funeral and outstanding-balance (schuldsaldo) cover across 11 insurers, energy comparison for electricity and gas (meter types, digital meter, solar panels and the prosumer tariff), telecom and mobile comparison, a budget barometer that benchmarks a household's fixed costs against Belgian averages, upload-your-proposal review, appointment booking (office or video call), callback requests, blog and FAQ content.
- **Mortgage brokerage in the office network** — about 70 hypotheek.winkel offices with hypotheek.experts who map the buyer's situation and project, compare the daily conditions of 25 banks, issue a feasibility certificate that buyers show to sellers, assemble the credit file, submit it to the chosen bank, follow approval, compromis and notary deed, and attach outstanding-balance and fire insurance; also refinancing, renovation loans (including the Flemish renovation obligation for low-EPC homes), second homes, new builds and buying as a self-employed person.
- **Insurance, energy and telecom brokerage** — quote, bind and switch flows run by customer-care advisors in Hasselt and Oujda by phone, chat and callback, including termination of the old contract (verzekeringen opzeggen), energy switches and telecom switches under BIPT Easy Switch.
- **Contract monitoring** — Peasy keeps an eye on the contracts it brokered, warns customers when a better or cheaper option exists and takes care of the small print; the cross-sell engine that turns one mortgage into a household relationship.
- **Network and partner operations** — lead routing to the nearest office, franchise and office management, production and conversion dashboards, the academy that trains scarce mortgage experts and insurance advisors, partner product catalogues and tariff feeds, and reconciliation of the referral commissions paid by more than 50 banks, insurers, energy suppliers and telecom operators, with payout to the office network.
- **Foundation** — identity and consent, FSMA-regulated advice records (credit and insurance intermediation), complaints and audit trail, GDPR, integrations with bank and insurer systems, e-signature, telephony and analytics.

**Adjacent scope (modeled lightly).** Consumer credit and credit grouping (a Meilleurtaux strength), business energy and telecom for small companies (Aanbieders.be "Bedrijven & Zakelijk"), charging stations, pension and investment products, and the post-acquisition integration with Meilleurtaux.

**Explicitly excluded.** Peasy is not a lender, insurer or energy supplier (contracts are always concluded with the partner; Peasy stays on the sidelines to prove its independence), not a bank branch network, not a real-estate agent or property portal, not a personal-finance budgeting app with account aggregation, and not a public price-comparison tool such as the VREG V-test or Wikifin. This distinguishes the domain from `real-estate-marketplace` (finding the home), `real-estate-erp-platform` (managing property portfolios), `energy-market-operations-platform` (supplying energy) and `payments-and-revenue-infrastructure` (moving money). Here the business is intermediation: comparison technology plus human advice, paid for by partner commissions, measured in appointments, files, contracts and household savings.

## Value exchange

- **Households** (first-time buyers, refinancers and second-home owners, families cutting fixed costs) get free, independent comparison across dozens of providers, human guidance through a once-in-a-decade decision, the paperwork done, and an ongoing watch on their contracts. They pay nothing extra: "of je nu rechtstreeks naar hen stapt of via ons, jij betaalt geen (pro)cent meer".
- **Mortgage experts and franchisees** get a brand that generates appointments, a workbench that compares 25 banks in one screen, a file process that banks accept quickly, training and certification, and a commission share; scarce-skill roles are grown in-house because the market cannot supply them.
- **Customer-care advisors** get comparison modules, scripts and switch execution tooling that let them resolve most requests in one contact.
- **Banks, insurers, energy and telecom partners** get qualified, well-documented demand at a predictable acquisition cost and pay a referral fee per concluded contract.
- **The strategic asset** is the combination of digital reach (three comparison brands in two languages), physical trust (70 offices and 150+ experts with a 4.8/5 Trustpilot rating for hypotheek.winkel), the resulting household relationship across mortgage, insurance, energy and telecom, and the data to know when each household can save again.

## Strategic spine (1/3/5-year horizons)

- **Year 1 — One front door, integrated with Meilleurtaux.** Consolidate three brands behind Peasy, align internal and operational structures with Meilleurtaux (knowledge sharing, shared bank and insurer relationships, 81 offices nationwide), add consumer credit to the offer, and lift expert productivity with a bank-ready file process while mortgage volumes recover.
- **Year 3 — The household relationship, not the transaction.** A monitored contract vault per household with proactive re-comparison for insurance, energy and telecom, cross-sell from every mortgage, a national bilingual advisory network fed by one lead engine, and an academy that makes advisor supply a planned capacity rather than a bottleneck.
- **Year 5 — Belgium's household-finance platform.** AI-assisted advice preparation and file assembly supervised by experts, partner APIs for real-time pricing and binding, savings, investment and pension products through the Meilleurtaux group, and a pan-European marketplace position.
