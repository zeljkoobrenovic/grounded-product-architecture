# Enterprise Architecture Management Platform

This domain models the enterprise architecture management software market in which Ardoq, SAP LeanIX, Bizzdesign, OrbusInfinity, and Avolution ABACUS operate.

The selected domain is the best fit because all named products compete around a common customer problem: helping enterprises connect strategy, portfolios, business capabilities, applications, technology standards, risk, roadmaps, and execution decisions in a continuously updated architecture model. The domain intentionally includes adjacent strategic portfolio management, application portfolio management, technology risk management, solution architecture, business architecture, and transformation governance capabilities when they materially shape buyer expectations.
