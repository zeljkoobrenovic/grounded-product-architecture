# Internet Number Registry and Routing Trust

This domain models the RIPE NCC core product area around Internet number resource registration, member services, routing security, DNS authority, policy/community coordination, and Internet measurement data.

The selected scope is more suitable than a generic DNS, DDoS, or infrastructure-services domain because RIPE NCC's core mandate is to operate as a Regional Internet Registry for Europe, the Middle East, and parts of Central Asia. Adjacent DNS, DDoS, CDN, and IP address marketplace providers are included in the competitive landscape because they shape the surrounding trust, routing, registry, and operational tooling expectations.

Primary reference examples used for structure:

- `public-cloud-services`
- `cloud-data-and-ai-platform`
- `enterprise-architecture-management-platform`

Public information is strong for RIPE NCC's service scope, membership statistics, charging model, RPKI coverage, and peer RIR role. Some operational targets in the customer, scorecard, and objective files are modeled assumptions calibrated from public RIPE NCC member updates and comparable infrastructure-service operating metrics.
