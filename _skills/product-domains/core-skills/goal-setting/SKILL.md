# Goal Setting

## Purpose

Turn strategy into objectives that can guide execution and be reviewed. Goals should be outcome-based, measurable, and connected to customer value, business value, and implementation work.

## Workflow

1. Start from product strategy horizons and KPI architecture.
2. Define objectives for current, next, KTLO, and archived horizons when the domain uses those folders.
3. Write objective narratives around customer or business outcomes, not activity.
4. Attach measurable success criteria and supporting initiatives where supported.
5. Balance ambition with realism given roadmap, team ownership, dependencies, and evidence.

## Quality Bar

- Goals use concrete KPI leaves, not broad categories.
- Objectives can be evaluated without reading the author's intent.
- Goals are coherent across customer, business, platform, trust, and operational needs.
- KTLO goals protect reliability, compliance, cost, and system health where relevant.

## Repository Fit

- Target folders: `_config/product-domains/<domain>/objectives/current`, `next`, `ktlo`, and `archived`.
- Link objectives to initiatives, discoveries, releases, teams, and product bricks where schema supports it.

## Avoid

- Output goals such as "improve experience" with no measurable target.
- Confusing roadmap deliverables with outcomes.
- Creating only growth goals while ignoring trust, reliability, or operations.
