# Strategic Planning

## Purpose

Translate strategy into a coherent sequence of choices over time. Strategic planning should clarify what matters now, what should be prepared next, and what becomes possible later.

## Workflow

1. Start from product strategy, KPI architecture, customer journeys, and business model.
2. Identify planning horizons and the assumptions behind each horizon.
3. Sequence milestones by value, dependency, risk reduction, market timing, and operational readiness.
4. Explicitly connect phases to capabilities, product bricks, teams, and releases.
5. Include learning milestones where uncertainty is high.

## Quality Bar

- The plan shows why sequence matters.
- Near-term work is concrete; later horizons are directional but still meaningful.
- Dependencies and risks are visible.
- Strategic milestones are not just feature batches.
- The plan balances customer value, business outcomes, trust, operations, and platform readiness.

## Repository Fit

- Planning appears in customer product strategies, objectives, roadmap files, release plans, and product-brick targets.
- Use the repository's current/next/KTLO/archive structure where present.

## Avoid

- Treating all ideas as parallel priorities.
- Writing 3-year or 5-year horizons as fantasy end states without intermediate proof.
- Ignoring foundational capabilities needed before customer-facing outcomes can scale.
