# Value Proposition Design

## Purpose

Define the customer-specific reason to choose the product. A value proposition connects pains, gains, jobs, outcomes, differentiation, and proof points without becoming marketing copy.

## Workflow

1. Start from a specific customer segment and JTBD.
2. Identify the pains removed, gains created, risks reduced, and outcomes improved.
3. Distinguish functional, economic, trust, social, operational, and compliance value where relevant.
4. Link value claims to measurable KPIs or observable journey improvements.
5. Validate that the proposition explains why this offer wins against alternatives or doing nothing.

## Quality Bar

- The proposition is segment-specific and outcome-based.
- Claims are plausible given research and product capabilities.
- Benefits map to concrete product capabilities or delivery experiences.
- The value case includes adoption effort, switching costs, or trust barriers when material.

## Repository Fit

- Use value propositions to sharpen `customers/customers.json`, product strategy horizons, product deployments, and business scorecards.
- Keep language useful in static documentation, not sales-heavy.

## Avoid

- Generic claims such as "easy to use", "innovative", or "best-in-class" without context.
- Listing features instead of outcomes.
- Claiming differentiation that public evidence or category structure does not support.
