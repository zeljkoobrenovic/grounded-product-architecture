# Business Model Design

## Purpose

Explain the economic engine behind the product domain. The model should clarify who pays, what triggers revenue, what drives cost, how value is exchanged, and which operating metrics matter.

## Workflow

1. Identify participants: payers, users, suppliers, partners, internal operators, and regulators.
2. Describe revenue streams, pricing logic, contract model, marketplace take rate, subscription tiers, usage pricing, services, or advertising where relevant.
3. Map business outcomes to customer outcomes and operating constraints.
4. Identify unit economics drivers such as liquidity, utilization, fulfillment cost, gross margin, churn, expansion, risk loss, or support effort.
5. Use only sourced metrics or clearly labeled assumptions.

## Quality Bar

- The model explains how value capture follows from product use.
- Multi-sided economics are explicit when the domain is a marketplace or platform.
- Operating costs and constraints are not ignored.
- Business KPIs can be connected to KPI architecture and objectives.

## Repository Fit

- Business model assumptions inform `business/scorecard.json`, `business/competition.json`, product strategy, objectives, and roadmap sequencing.
- Keep metrics scoped to what sources report.

## Avoid

- Treating monetization as a single revenue label.
- Inventing pricing or margins without evidence.
- Ignoring non-revenue value in internal or public-sector domains.
