# Product Strategy

## Purpose

Translate customer needs and business realities into a directional strategy that can guide objectives, roadmap, delivery, capabilities, product bricks, and team ownership.

## Workflow

1. Start with the target customer groups and the domain boundary.
2. Define the product vision in terms of customer outcomes and business viability.
3. Identify differentiation, strategic focus, product themes, and major tradeoffs.
4. Create 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year horizons with focus, product theme, customer KPI, business KPI, and milestones.
5. Ensure the strategy can be traced to capabilities, product bricks, delivery flows, and objectives.

## Quality Bar

- Strategy is specific to the domain, not generic growth language.
- Horizons show sequencing, not the same theme repeated three times.
- Customer KPIs and business KPIs are concrete and measurable.
- Milestones are plausible for the maturity and category.
- Strategy acknowledges constraints such as trust, regulation, liquidity, integration, reliability, or operations where relevant.

## Repository Fit

- Target location is usually `productStrategy` inside `customers/customers.json`.
- Strategy should feed objectives, roadmap, product deployments, delivery releases, and product-brick investment state.
- Treat `productStrategy` as expected for mature customer groups.

## Avoid

- Vision statements that could apply to any company.
- Time horizons with no measurable outcome.
- Strategy that ignores implementation-facing bricks or delivery realities.
