# Goal Setting Skills

## Purpose

Apply the goal-setting cluster when converting product strategy into measurable outcomes, KPI trees, objectives, targets, and decision-ready success criteria.

## Core Skills To Combine

- `core-skills/kpi-architecture`
- `core-skills/goal-setting`
- `core-skills/product-strategy`
- `core-skills/strategic-planning`
- `core-skills/evidence-based-modeling`

## Workflow

1. Define customer and business north-star outcomes.
2. Build KPI pyramids with meaningful branches and measurable leaves.
3. Calibrate targets from evidence or explicit assumptions.
4. Convert strategy into objectives for current, next, KTLO, and archived horizons as appropriate.
5. Link goals to initiatives, releases, teams, customers, and product bricks where supported.

## Quality Bar

- KPI leaves are specific, measurable, and useful for decisions.
- Objectives describe outcomes, not activity lists.
- Leading, lagging, quality, risk, and diagnostic metrics are balanced.
- Targets are credible for the category and maturity.

## Repository Fit

- Primary targets: `customers/customers.json`, `objectives/**`, `business/scorecard.json`, and roadmap/release files.
- Keep KPI names consistent across the domain.

## Avoid

- One-child KPI trees and vague terminal metrics.
- Goals with no owner, no measure, or no customer/business relevance.
- Unsourced precision that implies false certainty.
