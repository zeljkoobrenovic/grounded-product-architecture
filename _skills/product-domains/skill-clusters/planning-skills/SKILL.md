# Planning Skills

## Purpose

Apply the planning cluster when strategy must become sequenced work: milestones, releases, initiatives, MVP scope, dependencies, learning loops, and expansion paths.

## Core Skills To Combine

- `core-skills/strategic-planning`
- `core-skills/roadmap-design`
- `core-skills/delivery-model-design`
- `core-skills/product-brick-architecture`
- `core-skills/organizational-design`

## Workflow

1. Start from strategy horizons, objectives, capabilities, and product-brick dependencies.
2. Define MVP, migration, expansion, and KTLO paths.
3. Sequence initiatives and releases by value, risk, dependency, and operational readiness.
4. Attach teams, product bricks, customers, KPIs, and discoveries where schema supports it.
5. Validate that the plan is feasible for the team topology and domain maturity.

## Quality Bar

- Sequencing logic is explicit.
- Roadmap items are tied to outcomes or risk reduction.
- Dependencies are concrete and actionable.
- MVP scope is narrow enough to launch and broad enough to prove value.
- Expansion paths explain what becomes possible next.

## Repository Fit

- Primary targets: `delivery/releases.json`, `objectives/*/initiatives.json`, `product-bricks/roadmap/roadmap.json`, and product-brick targets/documents.
- Inspect current generator expectations before changing source filenames.

## Avoid

- Roadmaps that are feature lists with no strategic order.
- Release plans disconnected from team ownership.
- Deferring all platform, data, trust, or operations work until after launch.
