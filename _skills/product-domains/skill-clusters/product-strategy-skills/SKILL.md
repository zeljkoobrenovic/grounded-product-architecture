# Product Strategy Skills

## Purpose

Apply the product strategy cluster when the work needs a coherent strategy anchored in customer segments, jobs, value propositions, business model, horizons, KPIs, and product themes.

## Core Skills To Combine

- `core-skills/customer-segmentation`
- `core-skills/jobs-to-be-done-modeling`
- `core-skills/customer-journey-design`
- `core-skills/value-proposition-design`
- `core-skills/business-model-design`
- `core-skills/product-strategy`
- `core-skills/strategic-planning`

## Workflow

1. Define customer segments and their materially different contexts.
2. Model jobs, journeys, pains, gains, and success criteria.
3. Express segment-specific value propositions and business model logic.
4. Define vision, differentiation, themes, and 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year horizons.
5. Connect strategy to KPIs, objectives, capabilities, product bricks, and roadmap.

## Quality Bar

- Strategy is grounded in customer progress and business viability.
- Customer, business, and implementation models do not contradict each other.
- Product themes are specific enough to guide capability and roadmap decisions.
- Horizons show real sequencing and learning.

## Repository Fit

- Primary target: `customers/customers.json`, especially customer groups, JTBD, journeys, KPI pyramids, and `productStrategy`.
- Strategy should also shape objectives, delivery, product bricks, and teams.

## Avoid

- Starting with internal features before customer segmentation.
- Product strategy that lacks measurable outcomes.
- Treating all segments as if they buy, adopt, and evaluate the product the same way.
