# Market Research Skills

## Purpose

Apply the market research cluster when the model needs external grounding: category structure, competitors, substitutes, customer behavior, monetization, operating constraints, and public evidence.

## Core Skills To Combine

- `core-skills/market-research`
- `core-skills/competition-analysis`
- `core-skills/evidence-based-modeling`
- `core-skills/business-model-design`
- `core-skills/product-domain-framing`

## Workflow

1. Start with official and authoritative sources.
2. Define category boundaries, direct competitors, substitutes, regional leaders, and ecosystem players.
3. Preserve sourced metrics with scope, period, title, and URL.
4. Infer customer groups, operating model, monetization, and product capabilities from public signals.
5. Record assumptions clearly where public information is incomplete.

## Quality Bar

- Official sources are preferred for company facts and metrics.
- Competition analysis includes comparability caveats.
- Research implications are translated into domain model choices.
- No invented business stats appear in source JSON.

## Repository Fit

- Primary targets: `business/competition.json`, `business/scorecard.json`, customer model, product strategy, and product-brick assumptions.
- Use research to justify domain selection and scope in summaries or notes.

## Avoid

- Using generic market reports as a substitute for official company evidence.
- Treating marketing claims as measured outcomes.
- Omitting substitutes that materially shape customer choice.
